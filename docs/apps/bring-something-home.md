# Bring Something Home

**Every life is temporary. What you bring home can outlast it.**

Bring Something Home is the separate cooperative bullet-hell RPG in
`~/personal/astro-emberwilds`. It is distinct from AJ's original, deployed Ember
Wilds. The canonical game owner completed this rebrand after coordination
authorized by AJ. Its focus is shared expeditions, readable projectile patterns,
personal loot, and the difference between carried and banked progress.

The browser predicts movement and replays unacknowledged inputs after server
reconciliation. The authoritative realm owns combat, loot and progression;
SQLite transactions preserve the results that survive an expedition. The
portfolio describes this architecture without claiming production-scale MMO
capacity or a public deployment.

The final coherent **1.4.0** distribution is
`../data/portfolio-builds/2026-09-05/bring-something-home-1.4.0.tar.gz`, SHA-256
`50a0d5d2d9d3a9f89816711a1386281a09320961558b3621b565a2acc560f5a7`.
All 53 regular files in the independent frozen runtime match the archive
byte-for-byte; production dependencies were installed separately from its lock.
The rebrand preserves the gameplay and compatibility files, storage keys and
database format. No original account data was opened or migrated.

The **game owner's fresh branded release checks** passed the production build,
94 unit tests, 28 native Chrome scenarios, five focused software-rendering
scenarios and six responsive viewports. The owner also verified a clean archive
installation, server health, assets, operator tools and SQLite backup. Earlier
depth, journey and load results belong to the pre-rebrand mechanics baseline;
they were not rerun merely to change the title. Early portfolio checks on the
obsolete 1.3 snapshot are superseded by this coherent release.
