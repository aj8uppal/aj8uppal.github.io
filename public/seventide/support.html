Seventide

Support

Seventide is a 3D strategy game on one contested hex island. Need help or want to report a problem?

Contact

Email aj8uppal@gmail.com. Please include your device and iOS or browser version, and, for an online room problem, the room code.

Getting started

  • Solo: choose a solo game on the start screen to play against bots. It works with no internet connection, in the app and (once loaded) in the browser.
  • Pass and play: play with friends on one device.
  • Online: create a room, share the invite link or the room code, and friends join with it. Online play needs an internet connection.

Common questions

I cannot join an online room. Check the code for typos. Rooms expire after inactivity (a lobby after 1 day, a match after 3 days, a finished match after 7 days). Ask the host to create a new room.

I closed the app mid-game. Local games resume from the start screen. For online games, reopen the app on the same device and rejoin the room from the start screen, or open the invite link again.

Is my data collected? No accounts and no tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

Someone used an offensive name in an online room. Names with slurs or crude words are refused automatically. If one gets through, email us the room code and the name and we will remove the room.

The game runs slowly. Try closing other apps, and tell us the device model so we can look into it. On an older iPhone or iPad, Settings › Graphics › Edges: Smooth uses less memory than Sharp.
