cursorweather

Your mouse has been leaving weather behind its whole life.
Now you can see it.

Chase five seconds of targets. We measure only how the pointer travelled — speed, curvature, tremor, hesitation, corrections, clicks — then paint those numbers as a sky.

Keyboard: press → or WASD to fly the pointer, space to click — it makes its own, visibly different weather. Enter starts.

Contains brief lightning flashes. Calm mode removes them.

Everything is measured and drawn on this device. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server to upload to.
