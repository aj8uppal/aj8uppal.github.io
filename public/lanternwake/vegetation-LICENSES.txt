vegetation-coastal-tree.glb
Source: Island Tree 02
Asset page: https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02
Primary metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/info/island_tree_02
Primary download manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/island_tree_02
Authors: Rob Tuytel (scanning, processing); Rico Cilliers (cleanup, processing).
Source license: CC0 1.0 Universal.
Modifications: selected the source tree; reduced trunk and branch geometry;
deterministically sampled and simplified individual source leaf blades while
preserving their orientations and photo UVs; restored the author's leaf alpha
mask; resized/compressed PBR textures; grounded and centered the model; exported
as a meshopt-compressed GLB. Near model: 24,927 triangles. Far model: 6,299
triangles, with sampled complete leaf blades and reduced woody branches. Groups
Tree_LOD0 and Tree_LOD1 each contain three material parts, sharing PBR textures.
The source identifies this as a coastal island tree, not a botanical olive.

vegetation-coastal-shrub.glb
Source: Wild Rooibos Bush, largest source variant wild_rooibos_bush_a
Asset page: https://polyhaven.com/a/wild_rooibos_bush
Primary metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/info/wild_rooibos_bush
Primary download manifest: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/wild_rooibos_bush
Authors: James Ray Cock (modeling); Jenelle van Heerden (photography).
Source license: CC0 1.0 Universal.
Modifications: selected one of the five source variants; restored the author's
leaf/twig alpha mask; resized/compressed PBR textures; grounded and centered the
model; exported as a meshopt-compressed GLB. Near model: 13,151 triangles. Far
model: 2,796 triangles, with sampled complete leaf/twig cards and reduced wood.
Groups Shrub_LOD0 and Shrub_LOD1 each contain three parts sharing PBR textures.
The source plant is from South Africa's Karoo/Namaqualand; this
game uses its dry scrub form and does not represent it as Mediterranean native.

The source geometry and photographic textures were not generated by AI.
Processing scripts and browser inspection artifacts are under .oneshot/.
