Bundled maps are the original 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL tangent-space normal,
and roughness maps from Poly Haven, totaling 745,086 bytes:
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/fine_grained_wood/fine_grained_wood_col_1k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/fine_grained_wood/fine_grained_wood_nor_gl_1k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/fine_grained_wood/fine_grained_wood_rough_1k.jpg

Integration: local bundled files, shared by the ship fleet. The scanned grain
follows each strake, spar and rail, using geometry UVs at a restrained 0.8m
tile size. Shader caulk lines preserve the hull strakes and staggered 28cm deck
planks. Board-to-board color variation, gentle normal strength and minimum
roughness preserve a weathered working surface without glossy varnish.
The deck has a lighter, slightly desaturated finish. Original canvas stitching,
ship geometry, rigging, animations and upgrades are unchanged.
