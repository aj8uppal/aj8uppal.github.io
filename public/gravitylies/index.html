Why a phone can feel gravity at all. An accelerometer measures proper acceleration — acceleration relative to free fall. A phone lying still on a table is not floating; the table is shoving it upward, so the sensor honestly reads about 1 g pointing up. Down is simply the opposite of that. In free fall the same sensor reads zero.

Honest about the number. This is a live estimate, not a survey instrument. The gravity vector is a complementary blend of a low-pass filtered accelerometer signal and the browser’s reported orientation angles; hand tremor, linear motion, sensor bias and the two-second flat calibration put roughly ±3° of slop on it, more while you are moving. In demo mode the tilt is synthetic and labelled as such.
