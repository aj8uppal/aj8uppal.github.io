A toy measure of how two people tapped a screen. It is not a measurement of your lungs, your health, or your relationship.

how this works

Two people, one screen. Each person takes one side. Press when you breathe in, release when you breathe out. Keep your own rhythm — comfortable and slow.

The creature's left half is fed by the left person, the right half by the right person. It only becomes whole and symmetric as your two rhythms drift into the same speed and the same moment.

Two soft tones follow you. When they stop wobbling against each other, you are close.

Touch: hold opposite edges of the phone with a thumb each. Keyboard: left person holds F, right person holds J.

Hold together for about three breaths and the creature hatches. There is no losing here — if it doesn't happen yet, it just keeps breathing with you.

Everything runs on this device. No microphone, no camera, no network, no storage of anything you do.

serve me over http

This page is built from ES modules, which browsers refuse to load from a file:// address. From the folder above this one, run:

python3 -m http.server 8900

then open http://localhost:8900/samebreath/. Still entirely offline — the server is only there to satisfy the browser's origin rules.
