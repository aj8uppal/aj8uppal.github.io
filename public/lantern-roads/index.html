    The Lantern Roads

    a quiet road, a lit lantern, and whoever you meet along it

    The Lantern Roads is played with a keyboard or a gamepad, so it is best on a computer. There are no touch controls yet.

    WASD / arrows move · Shift run · E talk / use · J or click strike · K dodge · L or right-click Ember Bolt · M map · H guide

    Keeper guide

    Controls

    Your objective

    The panel at the top left always shows it. This is a reference: the world keeps running.

    Finding your way

    A glowing lantern floats over what you are looking for. When it is off screen, an arrow at the edge of the view points toward it and a gold "→" label names the exit to take.

    Press the map key (M) to see the scenes and roads, where you are, where the objective is, and which roads are still closed to you.

    Walk close and use the interaction shown to talk, pick something up, light a road lantern or travel.

    The road lanterns

    The Hollow is a black tide that hollows the living. Hearthfire burns it. Lighting a road lantern pushes the dark back for good: it is safe ground, it restores your health and Lumen, and it is where you wake if you fall. Nothing is lost when you do.

    Pad: A south, B east, X west, Y north. Left stick / D-pad scroll this guide; B or Back closes it. The world keeps running while you read.
